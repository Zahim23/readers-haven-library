Digital Services

Everything you need beyond the bookshelf – available in the library and online.

Online Catalogue

Available 24/7

  1. Open the catalogue page on the library kiosk or your phone.
  2. Search by title, author or subject.
  3. Reserve a book and collect it within 3 days.

E-Books

Available 24/7 for members

  1. Log in with your membership number.
  2. Choose a title from the e-book shelf.
  3. Read online or download for 14 days.

Study-Room Booking

9:00 AM – 7:00 PM, 2-hour slots

  1. Ask at the front desk or book at the kiosk.
  2. Pick a free room and time slot.
  3. Show your membership card when you arrive.

Printing & Scanning

During opening hours

  1. Send your file to the library print station.
  2. Pay PKR 5 per black-and-white page at the desk.
  3. Scanning to e-mail is free for members.

Free Wi-Fi

During opening hours

  1. Connect to the network named RH-Guest.
  2. Ask the front desk for the daily password.
  3. Please keep downloads small so everyone can use it.
