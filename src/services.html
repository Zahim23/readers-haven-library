Explore Our Smart Library Services

Readers' Haven brings learning and technology together. Discover simple digital tools designed to make your library experience easier, faster and more enjoyable.

Smart Book Discovery

Online Access: 24/7

Find your next favourite book without searching every shelf.

  1. Visit the digital catalogue using your phone or library computer.
  2. Enter a book title, writer's name or topic of interest.
  3. Check availability and request a copy for collection within 4 days.

Digital Reading Corner

Members: Anytime Access

Enjoy reading wherever you are through our digital collection.

  1. Access the digital shelf using your library membership details.
  2. Explore educational books, novels and learning resources.
  3. Select an e-book and enjoy a 10-day digital borrowing period.

Focus Zone Reservations

10:00 AM – 6:00 PM | 90-Minute Sessions

Reserve a quiet space for focused study or group discussions.

  1. Visit the reception counter to check available study spaces.
  2. Select your preferred room and available session.
  3. Present your membership card before starting your session.

Document Support Station

Available During Library Hours

Get your academic documents printed or scanned conveniently.

  1. Bring your document on a USB drive or send it to the print desk.
  2. Black-and-white printing costs PKR 8 per page.
  3. Members can scan up to 5 pages free per visit.

Learning Connect Wi-Fi

Free During Library Hours

Stay connected while researching, studying or completing assignments.

  1. Enable Wi-Fi on your laptop, tablet or smartphone.
  2. Select the library guest network and request access details at reception.
  3. Use the connection responsibly to support a smooth experience for everyone.
