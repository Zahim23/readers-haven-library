Welcome to Readers' Haven

Our mission is to make books, learning and digital resources free and easy to reach for every resident of the uni – from first-time readers to lifelong learners.

Our Mission

Readers' Haven brings the community together through reading. We offer a quiet space to study, a growing collection of books, and digital services for the modern reader.

Location

Two minutes' walk from the Model Town bus stop.
