Membership Plans

Join Readers' Haven and borrow books for the whole year. All fees are in Pakistani Rupees (PKR) per year.

PlanAnnual FeeBorrowing LimitLoan Period
StudentPKR 1,5004 books14 days
AdultPKR 2,5006 books21 days
Family (up to 4 people)PKR 3,70012 books21 days

Late-Return Rules

  • A late fee of PKR 20 per book per day applies to Student plans.
  • A late fee of PKR 25 per book per day applies to Adult and Family plans.
  • Borrowing is paused until overdue books are returned and fees are paid.
  • Loans can be renewed once, for the same period, if no one has reserved the book.
  • Lost or damaged books must be replaced or paid for at the current price.
